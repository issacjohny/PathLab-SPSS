Dear SPSS Community manager,

I would like to request review of PathLab for IBM SPSS Statistics for possible inclusion as a Community extension in the SPSS Extension Hub.

PathLab provides an Analyze-menu interface for observed-variable mediation, moderation and conditional process analysis. Users assign variables, choose model stages and output options, and run an analysis through a native dialog. The package supports parallel, serial and combined mediator presets, paired-case percentile bootstrap intervals, conditional path inference, interaction diagrams and Johnson–Neyman diagrams for applicable continuous moderators. It creates SPSS Viewer tables and diagram output and can save numerical results and PNG copies locally.
