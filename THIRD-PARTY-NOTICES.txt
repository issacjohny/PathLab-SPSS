Scope of these notes

The SPE and Windows release ZIP contain PathLab source and support files.
They do not contain the numerical libraries' wheel binaries. The separate
setup step downloads dependencies from PyPI into the user's PathLab folder.
The dependency versions and their full license files remain with that
installation. IBM SPSS Statistics and its bundled Python are provided
separately and retain their own terms and credits.

the full notices from the exact distributions being redistributed. No such
binary redistribution is added by this submission preparation.

Statistical source provenance

source-provenance.json identifies the frozen source snapshot from the
Stratistics repository and the import adaptation made for SPSS. The original
source hash and vendored hash are retained. The numerical engine, model
definitions and exception contract in 0.2.2 are unchanged from the recorded
0.2.0 runtime. The independent test suite uses matrix-based statistical
references. It does not establish equivalence to the PROCESS macro.
